How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Click the "Send Account Deletion Request" button above.
  2. Your default email application will open automatically.
  3. The recipient (ali@read.top) and email subject will already be filled in.
  4. Please include your registered email address, phone number, username, or user ID so we can verify ownership of the account.
  5. Once verified, we will process your deletion request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your TopRead account.
  • Profile information.
  • Login information.
  • Registered email address or phone number.
  • Reading history.
  • Reading progress.
  • Bookmarks and favorites.
  • Application preferences associated with your account.
  • Other personal information linked to your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security investigations
  • Accounting or tax obligations

Unless a longer retention period is required by law, any retained information will normally be deleted within 180 days after your account deletion request has been completed.

Processing Time

After your identity has been verified, account deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed after completion.
